title: About Still
subtitle: About the theme
description: A quiet, typography-first Hexo theme for personal blogs.
---

Still is designed for essays, field notes, journals, and personal sites where words come first.

It includes responsive post and archive layouts, local search, light and dark modes, reading time, feed discovery, optional Giscus comments, and English and Simplified Chinese interface text.

The theme has no web-font, analytics, image-library, or front-end-framework dependency.

[View the source on GitHub](https://github.com/simple-is-awesome/hexo_theme_still).
